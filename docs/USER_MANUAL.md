# User manual

For trainees, consultants and coordinators. Administrators should read
[ADMIN_MANUAL.md](ADMIN_MANUAL.md).

---

## Signing in

Use your institutional training account. If two-factor authentication is enabled you
will be asked for a six-digit code after your password; recovery codes work in its
place and each can be used once.

**Install RTC on your phone.** In your browser menu choose *Install app* or *Add to
Home Screen*. Installed, it opens instantly and works without signal — which is the
point.

## For trainees

### Your dashboard

The number at the top left is your overall training score: the weighted progress of
the requirements your curriculum actually sets. Beside it is your promotion readiness
— the proportion of *mandatory* gates cleared, which is a different question from how
well you are doing.

**Performance by domain** shows where you stand across eight areas. A domain marked
*not assessed* means your curriculum sets no requirement there; it is excluded from
your overall score rather than counted as zero.

**What is outstanding** is the list to work from. Mandatory items block promotion;
advisory items do not, but they are visible to your supervisor.

### Recording activity

**Logbook → New entry.** It takes about twenty seconds.

- **Nothing counts until a consultant validates it.** A pending entry contributes
  nothing to any requirement or score.
- **Never enter patient identifiers.** Age, sex and diagnosis only. There is no field
  for a name or hospital number, and that is deliberate.
- **Your role matters more than the case count.** Assisting and performing
  independently are different requirements. Record what you actually did.
- **Write the reflection.** It is what turns a case list into evidence of learning,
  and it is what an examiner reads.

### Working offline

If you have no signal, record the entry anyway. It saves on your device and uploads
automatically when you reconnect. The header shows how many entries are queued.

Two things to know:

- Sign out and queued entries are lost. **Sync before signing out**, especially on a
  shared computer. The app blocks the "clear local data" button while anything is
  queued, but signing out does clear it.
- If a supervisor changed an entry while you were offline, your edit becomes a
  *conflict* rather than overwriting theirs. You will be shown both versions.

### If an entry is returned

A queried entry appears with your supervisor's comment. Amend it and it returns to
pending automatically. Repeated queries on the same entry are visible in your
professionalism signal, so read the comment before resubmitting.

### Competencies and EPAs

**Competencies** shows each competency with your current entrustment level against
your curriculum's target for this year. The outlined step on the scale is the target.

An unrated competency counts as zero — it never passes on silence. If something has
not been rated, ask to be assessed on it.

### Academic attendance

**Activities & CME.** Your percentage is measured against the mandatory sessions your
department actually held while you were in post, not against a fixed number. Use the
session code to check in; credits post immediately.

### Research

**Research** shows your dissertation's stage on a tracker and every milestone with its
due date. Most dissertations fail from drift, not from bad science — the tracker
exists so drift is visible early.

Your supervisor was allocated on expertise, availability, current workload, previous
supervision and declared conflicts of interest. The reasoning is recorded and you can
see it.

### Promotion

**Promotion** shows every gate and its result: requirements, minimum training time,
rotation closure, enrolment standing. If you are not recommended, the page says
exactly which gates failed and by how much. There is nothing hidden behind the verdict.

A committee can promote you against the engine's recommendation, and sometimes will —
but must record why.

## For consultants and supervisors

### Your dashboard

The figure that matters is **entries awaiting your sign-off**, with the age of the
oldest. Trainees cannot progress on unvalidated work, so a stale queue blocks people
who have done nothing wrong.

**Trainees who may need support** is generated from the analytics, not from any single
judgement. Treat it as a prompt for a conversation.

### The validation queue

**Validation queue.** Select the routine majority and validate in bulk; handle
anything needing a query individually.

- **Validate** — the entry counts from that moment and is locked. Neither of you can
  edit it afterwards, because it is evidence a college may inspect.
- **Query** — returns it for correction. A comment is required; the trainee sees it.
- **Reject** — for an entry that should not stand. Also requires a comment.

You can award entrustment ratings while validating, which is how most supervisors
actually work: judgement and evidence together.

### Assessments

**Assessments → new.** Your department's instruments are listed. Scores are weighted
per the instrument's design; a field you leave blank is excluded from both the
numerator and the denominator, so a partially-relevant encounter is not penalised.

Write the narrative feedback. The numbers age; the sentences are what the trainee
returns to.

### Closing a rotation

**Rotations → the trainee → Close.** The platform refuses to mark a rotation complete
while mandatory requirements are unmet. You can override with a comment — sometimes
the right call, when a trainee was redeployed or a service collapsed — and the
override is recorded.

## For coordinators

### Enrolling a trainee

**People** to confirm the account exists, then enrol them on a programme. The rotation
schedule generates automatically from the curriculum; preview it first — the preview
shows capacity warnings where a unit is already full.

### Managing the schedule

- **Extend** a rotation and every later planned posting shifts by the same period.
- **Remedial** creates a repeat posting linked to the one being remediated.
- **Approve leave** marked as extending training and the trainee's expected end date
  and remaining rotations move accordingly.

### Academic calendar

**Activities & CME → schedule.** Every session gets a check-in code. Mark sessions
mandatory only if attendance at them should count — the mandatory set is the
denominator of every trainee's attendance percentage.

## Common questions

**Why hasn't my score moved after logging ten cases?**
They are probably still pending validation. Only validated entries count.

**Why is a domain showing "not assessed"?**
Your curriculum defines no requirement in it. It is excluded from your overall score,
not counted against you.

**Why is my year-2 requirement showing almost nothing when I have logged hundreds of
cases?**
Year-scoped requirements measure only that year's window. If you are two months into
year 2, that is what is being measured.

**Can I delete an entry I made by mistake?**
Only before it is validated. Afterwards, ask your supervisor to query it.

**I am offline and the app shows old data.**
That is the cached copy from your last sync. New entries still save; everything
reconciles when you reconnect.

**I changed device — where is my data?**
On the server. Sign in and it downloads. Nothing is only ever on one device unless it
is still queued.
